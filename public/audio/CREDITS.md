Our edit makes a roughly 65.5-second loop: seconds 3–65 are followed by a
2.5-second crossfade joining the final three seconds to the first three seconds.
Loudness is normalized to -20 LUFS
with a -3 dB true-peak target, then encoded as stereo 44.1 kHz MP3. Resampling
does not add detail to the original 11.025 kHz recording. The recording plays
locally from the app; there are no streaming-provider requests during playback.
